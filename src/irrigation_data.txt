Evapotranspiration (ET0) measures water loss from soil and plants.

Crop coefficient (Kc) adjusts ET0 based on crop type.

Typical Kc values:
Corn: 0.8-1.2
Wheat 0.7-1.0
Vegetables: 0.5-1.0

Irrigation volume formula:
Volume = Area * Kc * ET0

Efficient irrigation reduces water waste and improves yield.
